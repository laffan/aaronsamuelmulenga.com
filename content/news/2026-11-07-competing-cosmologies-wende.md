---
title: "Exhibition — Competing Cosmologies: Interpreting the Sky"
date: 2026-11-07
---

A group exhibition at the Wende Museum in Culver City, California, November 7, 2026 – October 17, 2027, co-curated by the Wende's curatorial department and NASA historian Lois Rosson. The show looks at how space science from the Cold War onward shaped artists around the world, in four sections: Astronomy, the Space Race, Futurisms, and Aliens.

[View the exhibition at the Wende Museum](https://wendemuseum.org/exhibition/cosmos/)
