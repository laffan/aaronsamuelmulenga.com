*The Afronauts* (2020) is featured in *Colours of Africa*, an online exhibition by Design Indaba and Google Arts & Culture in which 60 African creatives each chose a colour to represent their home country. For Zambia, Mulenga chose copper — the country's leading export — tying it to hope, to the copper crosses once used as currency, and to Edward Mukuka Nkoloso's 1960s Zambian space programme.
