---
title: "Exhibition — Ukupupuka: An Exploration of Transcendence"
date: 2025-01-08
---

A solo exhibition at the Mary Porter Sesnon Art Gallery, UC Santa Cruz, January 8 – February 8, 2025. *Ukupupuka* means flight in Bemba. The show seeks visual avenues of elevation beyond physical limits, recovering the stories of marginalized Africans who took part in events like the First World War. In the *Afronauts* series, drawn from Edward Mukuka Nkoloso's 1964 space programme, the figures wear halos instead of helmets.
