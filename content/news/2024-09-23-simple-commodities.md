---
title: "Exhibition — Simple Commodities: Ba Zambia Twa Fika Pesa?"
date: 2024-09-23
---

A solo exhibition at the Lechwe Trust in Lusaka, curated by Sana Ginwalla, on view through October 3, 2024. Marking Zambia's sixty years of independence, the show opens with the installation *Ba, Zambia, twafika pesa?* — Zambia, where have we reached? — asking what freedom has brought amid the energy crisis and the rising cost of bread, mealie meal and fuel, and proposing an Africanfuturist way forward.
