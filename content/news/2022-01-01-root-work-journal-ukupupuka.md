An essay in *Root Work Journal*, Volume 2, Issue 1 — the *Summoning Flight* special issue. It traces transcendence, the spirit lifted beyond the body, through Eurocentric Christian iconography and Zambian cultural practice, setting objects of spiritual flight held in Zambian museums alongside Biblical texts about flight.
